  अनुवाद में" every time.

  "unchastity among women". That is fear content, it is what the channel exists
  not to be, and it runs into your note about not casting the shastra badly.
  It stays as the next-episode tease in `n11`.
- **Did not invent remedies.** `n6` says the chapter prescribes none, which is
  honest and still answers the question people are actually asking.

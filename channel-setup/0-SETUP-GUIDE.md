# Creating the channel — step by step

Work top to bottom. Roughly 30 minutes.
YouTube moves its menus around, so this describes *what to look for* rather than
where exactly to click.

---

## Step 1 — Create a Brand Account, not a personal channel

**This is the one step that is painful to undo. Get it right.**

1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account you want to own the channel
2. Click your picture (top right) → **Switch account** → **See all channels** →
   **Create a new channel**
3. Enter the name: `शास्त्र कथा` — this creates a **Brand Account**

**Why it matters.** A personal channel is welded to your Google account's own
name and can only ever have one owner. A Brand Account can be renamed freely,
can have several managers, and can be transferred to someone else or to a
company later. If the channel ever earns money, hires an editor, or gets sold,
you will need this. Converting afterwards is possible but tedious.

If YouTube ever shows your own name where the channel name should be, you are on
the personal channel. Back out and create the Brand Account.

---

## Step 2 — Name and handle

**YouTube Studio → Customisation → Basic info**

| field | value |
|---|---|
| Name | `शास्त्र कथा` |
| Handle | `@ShastraKathaIndia` |

`@shastrakatha` was already held — a 404 on the URL does not prove a handle is
free, because YouTube keeps reserved and dormant ones. Its own picker is the
only authority. The handle can be changed once every 14 days if a better one
frees up.

Keep the name in Devanagari. Your audience reads it, and it stands out in a feed
of Latin text. Latin appears in the handle anyway.

## Step 3 — Pictures

**Customisation → Branding**

- **Profile picture** → `1-avatar-800x800.png`
  (or `1-avatar-ALT-faceless.png` if you prefer the mandala)
- **Banner image** → `2-banner-2048x1152.png`

When the banner cropper appears, the name must sit inside the highlighted
"visible on all devices" box. It was composed for that box — if it looks right in
`2-banner-mobile-preview.png`, it is right.

- **Video watermark** → use the avatar again. It appears bottom-right during
  playback and subscribes on click.

## Step 4 — Description and links

**Customisation → Basic info**

Paste the channel description from `4-all-copy-paste-text.md` → *Channel
description (About)*. **Append the one-line disclaimer** from the Disclaimers
section of the same file.

Leave links empty until you have somewhere real to send people. An empty section
looks better than a dead Instagram.

---

## Step 5 — The settings people forget

**Settings (left sidebar, bottom) → Channel → Basic info**

- **Country of residence:** India — this affects monetisation eligibility later
- **Keywords:** paste the keyword block from `4-all-copy-paste-text.md`

**Settings → Upload defaults** — set these once and every upload inherits them:

- **Title / description:** paste the description template. Every upload starts
  with your disclaimer already in place instead of depending on memory.
- **Visibility:** Private. Deliberate — you want to check the render and captions
  before anything goes live. Flip each video to Public by hand.
- **Language:** Hindi
- **Category:** Education
- **Comments:** Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review. This subject
  attracts argument; you want a filter before you have time to moderate.
- **Licence:** Standard YouTube licence

**Settings → Advanced**
- "Do you want to set your channel as made for kids?" → **No, set this channel as
  not made for kids.** Answer honestly; it is not a children's channel, and a
  wrong answer here disables comments and costs you reach.

## Step 6 — Playlists

**Content → Playlists → New playlist.** Create the first one now, the rest as you
reach them:

1. `वास्तु शास्त्र` — description: `घर की दिशा, कमरों का स्थान — शास्त्र क्या कहते हैं`
2. `ज्योतिष शास्त्र` *(later)*
3. `योग शास्त्र` *(later)*
4. `त्योहार और परंपरा` *(later)*

One playlist per topic. It is how a viewer who liked one vastu video finds the
other nine.

---

## Step 7 — Before the first upload

- [ ] Brand Account, not personal
- [ ] Handle set to `@ShastraKathaIndia`
- [ ] Avatar and banner uploaded; name legible in the mobile safe area
- [ ] Description includes the disclaimer line
- [ ] Country India, language Hindi, category Education
- [ ] Not made for kids
- [ ] Upload defaults carry the description template
- [ ] `वास्तु शास्त्र` playlist exists
- [ ] Two-step verification on the Google account — a channel is worth stealing

**Verify the account** at `youtube.com/verify` before your first upload. It
unlocks custom thumbnails and videos longer than 15 minutes. Without it your
thumbnail will not upload and you will not know why.

---

## Step 8 — Upload day

Per video, from `4-all-copy-paste-text.md`:

1. Upload `out/<episode>/<episode>.mp4`
2. Title — Hindi, question form, under 60 characters
3. Description — the template, with `out/<episode>/chapters.txt` pasted under
   CHAPTERS. **It must start at `0:00`** or YouTube ignores the whole list.
4. Thumbnail — `3-thumbnail-template-1280x720.png`, re-rendered per episode
5. **Subtitles → upload `out/<episode>/<episode>.srt`.** Never rely on
   auto-captions; they mangle Devanagari and lakh/crore.
6. Add to the `वास्तु शास्त्र` playlist
7. End screen — previous video + subscribe
8. Publish, then pin the comment from `4-all-copy-paste-text.md`

---

## What not to do on day one

- **Do not upload all four videos at once.** Their views collapse against each
  other. Three or four at launch, then one every four days.
- **Do not buy subscribers.** It poisons the recommendation signal permanently.
- **Do not enable Shorts remixing** on videos whose art you want to keep control of.
- **Do not skip the `.srt`.** It is the cheapest reach you will ever get —
  search indexes it and auto-translate reads from it.

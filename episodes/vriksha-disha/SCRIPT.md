# Episode 2 — पेड़ और जल — script for review

Every line traces to a verse fetched and read on wisdomlib, quoted at the
bottom. 6 chapters, ~64 lines, ~55 nameable beats, ~4:00.

**Positive framing throughout.** 53.86, 53.77–78 and 53.119 all carry harm
lists (*"the children will die"*, *"the master of the house will die"*). None
is read aloud. Each is reported as *"the text advises against"*, and the
runtime goes to what to plant, where, when, and what to do instead of cutting.

**Pays off episode 1's promise.** Its last chapter said *"अगली बार, इसी अध्याय
में — जल का स्थान।"* Chapter 6 delivers 53.119's two auspicious directions.

## Sources — all fetched and read

**53.76** — "There will be misery if, opposite to the gate, there be roads,
trees, street corners, wells, pillars or gutters. **If these should be beyond a
distance of double the height of the house, they will produce no harm.**"

**53.85** — "…they will bring on prosperity if they be respectively situated in
the **north, east, south, and west** side of the house."

**53.86** — three categories advised against. *Harm clauses not read.*

**53.87** — "If it be felt inconvenient to remove the several trees mentioned
above, a number of **superior trees shall be grown between them**. These are —
Punnāga, Aśoka, Ariṣṭa, Bakula, Panasa, Śamī and Śāla."

**53.119** — north → "increase of wealth", north-east → "increase of sons".
*The other six clauses not read.*

**53.120** — "…other trees shall be cut down than those **in which birds
reside**, or that are broken or have dried or are burnt, or **in which the
Devas live** or which grow on cremation grounds…"

**53.121** — "**Cooked rice shall be offered and pūjā performed to the tree at
night**, and it shall be cut down **from left to right** next day. If it falls
on the **north or east** side, there will be increase of wealth."

**55.3** — "Ariṣṭa, Aśoka, Punnāga and Śirīṣa shall be grown either in gardens
or in houses by their seeds and they will **conduce to prosperity**."

**55.12** — "An interval of **twenty cubits** between trees is the best; one of
**sixteen** is passable; and one of **twelve** is injurious."

**55.31** — plant when the Moon passes **Mūla, Viśākhā, Puṣya, Śravaṇa, Aśvinī
or Hasta**.

## Two things I deliberately avoided

**No Hindi common names for Ariṣṭa or Punnāga.** Iyer glosses Ariṣṭa as the
soap-berry (रीठा) where it is usually नीम, and the appendix gives Punnāga as
कमीला where it is usually सुल्तानचंपा. Sanskrit names only for those two. The
other five are clean — बकुल, कटहल, शमी, साल, अशोक.

**No harm lists**, from any of the three verses that carry them.
